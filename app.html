Hang Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out nail heights, spacing and mat borders from the measurements you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know your wall, studs, anchors or hooks, or what a hook can hold, and it gives no verdict on whether any picture is safe to hang or looks right. Every number is your own entry.

The nail truth

The nail goes at the height of the wire peak, not at the center of the picture. Measure the wire drop with the wire pulled taut.

The row truth

Pictures of one width in a row with equal gaps, including the two ends. Left edges are measured from the left end of the wall.

The mat truth

The border a picture leaves inside a mat opening, with the picture centered.

Hang Math is exact arithmetic over the measurements you enter. Why it exists.
