Hang Math

A picture 18 tall centered 57 from the floor, with the wire 3 below the top, needs its nail at 63, not 57.

Boundary: arithmetic only Hang Math works out nail heights, spacing and mat borders from the measurements you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know your wall, studs, anchors or hooks, or what a hook can hold, and it gives no verdict on whether any picture is safe to hang or looks right. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: an 18 tall picture centered 57 up, 3 pictures 20 wide on a 100 wall, and an 11 by 14 picture in a 16 by 20 opening.

The nail truth

A picture 18 tall with its center 57 from the floor and the wire 3 below the top edge when pulled taut: put the nail 63 from the floor. The top edge sits at 66 and the bottom edge at 48.

The row truth

3 pictures 20 wide on a wall 100 wide leave 10 at each end and between pictures. Left edges at 10, 40, 70.

The mat truth

Opening 16 by 20 and picture 11 by 14: the borders are 2.5 on each side and 3 at the top and bottom.
